LRA, PLR, PSR: Dynamic Range

LRA measures loudness range, PLR peak-to-loudness, PSR peak-to-short-term. Learn targets for music, podcast, film.

LRA — Loudness Range

EBU R128 defines LRA as difference between 10th and 95th percentile of short-term loudness (3s window, > -70 LUFS). Music: 4-9 LU typical, Podcast: 4-7 LU, Film: 15-20 LU.

PLR — Peak to Loudness Ratio

PLR = dBTP - Integrated. Indicates how much headroom you left. Mastered pop: PLR 6-9, Dynamic jazz: PLR 12-16. Lower PLR = louder, more compressed.

PSR — Peak to Short-term Ratio

PSR = Short-term Max - Integrated. Shows punch of loudest 3s vs whole track. EDM drop PSR 6-9, Podcast PSR 4-6.

JSM Loudness calculates LRA per EBU, PLR and PSR instantly, 100% local.
