What is LUFS?

Brief: LUFS = Loudness Units relative to Full Scale — EBU R128 / ITU-R BS.1770 standard for perceived loudness. Unlike peak meters, LUFS uses K-weighting (high shelf at 1681.97 Hz + pre-filter at 38.13 Hz) and gating to match human hearing.
