What is LUFS?

Brief: LUFS = Loudness Units relative to Full Scale. How Spotify, YouTube, Apple measure how loud your track feels, not waveform height. Uses K-weighting mimicking your head. Target -14 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP true peak. -8 LUFS? Spotify turns DOWN 6dB, you lose punch.
