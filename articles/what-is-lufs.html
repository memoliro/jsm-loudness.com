What is LUFS?

Brief: LUFS = Loudness Units relative to Full Scale. It's how Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music measure how loud your track *feels*, not just how tall the waveform is. Unlike old RMS meters, LUFS uses K-weighting that mimics your head. Target -14 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP true peak. If you're -8 LUFS, Spotify turns you DOWN 6dB and you lose punch.

Why RMS fails

RMS weights all frequencies equally, no head model, no gating. Bass-heavy track reads louder in RMS but not perceived. K-weighting adds +4dB around 2-4kHz where ear sensitive. Gating ignores quiet intros. That's why old tools show -17.5 vs true -14.05.
