Why TikTok sounds different

TikTok transcodes to AAC 64-128kbps, loudness measurement on decoded AAC can be 0.3dB different. True peak after AAC can rise +0.8dB. That's why -1.0 dBTP headroom matters. Our waveform shows red dots >-1.0 dBTP before upload.

Device simulation

Car: high-pass 80Hz + bass shelf + cabin reverb. iPhone: mono sum, high-pass 250Hz, 2kHz boost. Bluetooth: low-pass 15kHz, 10ms jitter. Use our preview A/B: Play Original vs Play Fixed on each device to hear penalty before upload.
