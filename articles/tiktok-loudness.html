TikTok, Reels & Shorts Loudness

TikTok and Instagram Reels loudness target is -14 to -16 LUFS, -1 dBTP. Learn PLR and PSR for short-form, why mobile speakers need dynamics.

Why TikTok is different

Phone speakers compress heavily. A track with PLR 14 dB (loudness vs peak) sounds louder and clearer than a crushed -7 LUFS master with PLR 6 dB. TikTok normalizes to around -14 LUFS, but doesn't publish spec like Spotify.

Sweet spot tested: -14 to -16 LUFS integrated, -11 to -9 LUFS short-term max, PLR 8-13, PSR 6-10, dBTP -1. This keeps voice intelligible on phone.

PLR and PSR

PLR = Peak to Loudness Ratio = dBTP - Integrated LUFS. Low PLR = heavily compressed. PSR = Short-term Max - Integrated. For TikTok hook, PSR 5-8 dB keeps chorus punchy.

Workflow for short-form

  • Cut to 15-30s, measure integrated
  • If integrated -18 (too quiet), lift gain but keep true-peak -1
  • Check LRA < 6 LU for consistent phone playback
