Spotify Loudness: Why -14 LUFS is Target

Spotify Normalizes to -14 LUFS. Louder masters get turned down, not louder.

Spotify Modes

Normal -14, Loud -11, Quiet -19. 85% use Normal.

Why -14

Before normalization, everyone crushed to -6. Now -8 track turned down 6dB, loses punch. -14 stays untouched.

True-peak -1

Ogg Vorbis 320k adds 0.8dB overshoot. Target -1 dBTP.
