Spotify Loudness -14 LUFS

Brief: Spotify normalizes everything to -14 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP true peak. If your master is -8 LUFS, Spotify turns it DOWN -6dB (you lose punch, no loudness advantage). If it's -18 LUFS, Spotify may turn it UP (with limiter). Best is to master to -14.0 ±0.5 LUFS, -1.0 dBTP so Spotify does nothing.
