LRA, PLR, PSR - Loudness Range Explained

Brief: LRA = how much loudness changes in your song. Low LRA = tight, modern pop (3-6 LU). High LRA = dynamic, classical (9-15 LU). PLR = Peak to Loudness Ratio = True Peak minus Integrated. PSR = Short max minus Integrated. Your file is tight modern.

Your file: Dynamic Range 3.30 LU (lufs.org) LRA 3.4 LU (wutools) Integrated -14.05 Short -11.88 Mom -11.23 Relative -24.07 PLR 10.97 / PSR 8.80 — 3.3 LU = tight pop, good for streaming.

LRA calculation

Collect short-term 3s loudness values (1s hop) above absolute gate -70 and relative gate -10. Sort, P10 = 10th percentile, P95 = 95th percentile, LRA = P95 - P10. For HAYDI: P10 ~ -16.5 LUFS P95 ~ -13.2 LUFS LRA 3.3. If LRA >9, verse/chorus too different, Spotify may duck. If LRA <2, over-compressed, fatigue.
